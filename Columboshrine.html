Columbo Shrine

Columbo is a crime tv serie that ran from the 1970s until early 2000s. It is an episodic show where the AMAZING lieutenant Columbo catches the murderer. Columbo is one of my favorite characters ever and I may or may not have a crush on him (yeah I know, it's weird!!)

The thing that makes me love Columbo so much is how he already knows who the killer is the second he steps foot in those mansions. Columbo is not your classic "whodunit" show where we go on the journey with the protagonist to find out who the killer is. The twist here is that we see the murder happen in the beginning of the show, and after that we see how Columbo solves the mystery.
